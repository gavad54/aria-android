The 24 keys were an early subset, not the complete 26.0 module registry. The full registry contains 109 module keys: 90 exact matches, 15 semantic matches, and 4 unresolved. The complete archive crosswalk contains 986 rows, of which 842 are archive/engineering lineage and 144 are the 72 commercial offers plus 72 bridge rows. Therefore the full overlay is 639 + 986 = 1,625 rows.

## Productization rules
1. Do not delete or rename source IDs, source names, or historical relationships.
2. Exact/semantic historical matches inherit the canonical host; no duplicate billing.
3. Four unresolved historical module keys remain preserved but cannot be charged or counted as independent modules until a distinct contract and user value are proven.
4. Shared engines are versioned services with host modules, metering of internal compute/provider cost, authorization, persistence, observability and tests; not standalone products by default.
5. Commercial catalog offers keep their existing IDs, entitlements, metering and revenue descriptions. Current state is designed/not live; no payment or revenue is claimed.
6. Bridge records preserve catalog-to-canonical relationships and inherit the same offer entitlement; no second charge.
7. Value paths are end-to-end journeys through modules, not independent modules or fees.
8. Accounting/ERP remains outside ARIA and belongs to ARIANA.

## Operational status
This is a preservation-first productization design and mapping overlay. It does not claim E2E verification, connected payment providers, production activation, or live revenue.
